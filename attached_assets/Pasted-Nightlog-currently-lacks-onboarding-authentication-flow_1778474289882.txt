Nightlog currently lacks onboarding, authentication flow, and user identity creation, which makes the product feel incomplete and unrealistic.

Introduce a full product-entry experience before users enter the application.

---

## APPLICATION FLOW

Create the following architecture:

1. Public Marketing Website
2. Authentication + Onboarding Flow
3. Authenticated Product Application

---

## LANDING EXPERIENCE

The landing page should:

* introduce Nightlog,
* explain the AI nightlife concept,
* showcase screenshots,
* and transition users into the platform.

Primary CTA:

* Enter Nightlog
* Join the City
* Start Exploring

---

## AUTHENTICATION FLOW

Create cinematic authentication pages for:

* Login
* Signup
* Continue as Guest

The auth experience should feel:

* premium,
* immersive,
* nightlife-oriented,
* and socially driven.

Avoid generic authentication templates.

Use:

* layered backgrounds,
* atmospheric motion,
* cinematic lighting,
* and smooth transitions.

---

## ONBOARDING FLOW

After signup, introduce a multi-step onboarding flow.

Users should configure:

* username/handle,
* city,
* nightlife preferences,
* vibe preferences,
* favorite genres,
* social behavior,
* and nightlife goals.

Examples:

* underground
* rooftop
* ambient
* afterhours
* techno
* live music
* social
* intimate
* high energy
* cinematic nights

The onboarding should feel intelligent and personalized.

Add:

* animated progress,
* smooth transitions,
* adaptive recommendations,
* and AI personalization moments.

Example:
“Building your nightlife graph...”
“Calibrating city energy...”
“Learning your night patterns...”

---

## STATE + SESSION SYSTEM

Implement believable frontend-only session handling using:

* local storage,
* mock authentication,
* persistent onboarding state,
* saved preferences,
* and simulated user sessions.

Do not build a full backend auth system yet.

The goal is frontend realism and believable product UX.

---

## TRANSITION INTO APP

After onboarding, route users into:

* /app

The transition should feel immersive and intentional.

Entering the product should feel like:

* entering a live city nightlife network.

---

## DESIGN GOAL

The complete experience should now feel like:

* a real venture-backed consumer startup,
* with a cinematic public brand layer,
* sophisticated onboarding,
* and a dense realtime social product underneath.

Focus heavily on:

* frontend polish,
* onboarding UX,
* motion design,
* interaction quality,
* responsive behavior,
* and product realism.
